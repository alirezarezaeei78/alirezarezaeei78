## Research and education

I completed my **M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering (Control)** at **K. N. Toosi University of Technology** in 2026.

My thesis developed **GTS**, a customer next-purchase time prediction workflow combining dynamic features, co-purchase graph embeddings, an attention-based LSTM, and model stacking. Across five datasets with temporal leakage controls, the final configuration reduced MAE by **11.7–24.8% against naive rules**. The workflow was implemented in the ARAS NPD laboratory research prototype.

I have also worked on recommender-system research and served as a teaching assistant for Machine Learning and Linear Control.
